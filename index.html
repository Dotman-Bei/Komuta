Every seat has a history

Komuta is an open market for concert, stadium, flight and train seats you can't use. List one, take offers, and swap it in a single transaction on BOT Chain.

A seat you can't use is dead value

Airlines forfeit it. Railways forfeit it. Resale platforms take double-digit fees and hold your payout. Komuta gives the seat itself an open ledger.

One transaction, no escrow

  1. About two minutes

    First, describe the seat: what it is, when, where, the seat reference, and a price or swap-only.

  2. Up to 24 hours

    Then offers arrive. Each stays open for a day, and you can counter any of them.

  3. Under a second

    Last, accept. Seat and payment change hands together, or neither does.

The contract never holds your money between an offer and its acceptance.

Built around the seat

Three kinds of seat

Events, flights and trains share one listing: a date, a venue or route, a seat reference, and a price or swap-only.

History you can replay

Every listing, offer and swap is an event on BOT Chain. Rebuild any seat's chain of holders from the explorer and it matches the app, row for row.

Reputation nobody can buy

After each swap both sides rate each other once, one to five stars. The score lives in the contract and can't be transferred.

Fees of a fraction of a cent

A seat can change hands a dozen times for less than one resale fee, so even a seat worth under a dollar is worth listing.

Plain answers

Does Komuta get me through the door?

No. Komuta trades the right to a seat; the venue, airline or railway decides entry. Check their transfer rules before you buy.

Who holds my money during a swap?

Nobody. Payment and seat move in the same transaction, or neither moves. The contract never holds funds between an offer and its acceptance.

What happens to an offer nobody answers?

It expires after 24 hours. Accepting it then fails, and nothing is lost because nothing was locked.

Can the same seat be listed twice?

No. The contract allows one active listing per seat, and the app flags any listing with the same venue and seat reference.

What do I pay with?

BOT Chain's native token. No cards, no fiat. Listing and settling each cost a fraction of a cent.

Is my travel plan public?

Only the category, date, price and a hash of the venue or route go on-chain. The readable venue and seat stay in the listing's metadata, revealed by the holder.
